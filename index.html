BEFORE YOU PLAY

Heater is an educational and analytical tool. Card counting is not illegal in most places, but casinos can refuse service, ban players, or void winnings online. Using a phone or device at a live blackjack table is prohibited in many jurisdictions, including Nevada under NRS 465.075. You are responsible for knowing the laws and venue rules where you play. 18+ only.
